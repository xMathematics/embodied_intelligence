## 4. 参考文献——必读清单

**入门必读**：
1. Goodfellow (2016). *Deep Learning*. MIT Press.
2. LeCun, Bengio, Hinton (2015). Deep learning. *Nature*.

**SLAM具身**：
3. DeepMind. (2022). A generalist agent (GATO). *arXiv*.
4. Brohan et al. (2023). RT-2: Vision-language-action models.

**生成式AI**：
5. Ho et al. (2020). Denoising diffusion probabilistic models. *NeurIPS*.
6. Rombach et al. (2022). High-resolution image synthesis with latent diffusion models. *CVPR*.

**大模型**：
7. Vaswani et al. (2017). Attention is all you need. *NeurIPS*.
8. Brown et al. (2020). Language models are few-shot learners. *NeurIPS*.
